About

I'm a GIS professional and veteran with nearly a decade of experience building and managing geospatial systems for government and federal agencies. My background spans local government, federal contracting, and infrastructure compliance work, giving me a broad view of how GIS systems get built, used, and maintained.

That experience started with turning fragmented, inconsistent data into systems people could actually rely on: clean data models, cartographic standards, and automation that removed repetitive work. It's since grown into leading full GIS Enterprise deployments end to end, architecture, governance, and the public-facing applications built on top of them. I also teach GIS at the college level, mentoring the next generation of practitioners.

I'm ready for my next challenge, and genuinely excited about what that looks like, whether that's modernizing a GIS Enterprise system from the ground up or leading a team through one already in motion.
