3D Print Cost Calculator

A single-file, client-side calculator for pricing custom 3D print orders, built to cost out multi-color print jobs and compare pricing strategies before quoting a customer or a friend.

TYPETool / Web App
STACKHTML, CSS, Vanilla JS
DEPENDENCIESNone, no build step
CONTEXT3D print shop, Bambu P1S

Overview

This tool grew out of pricing custom print orders, novelty models, coins, and keepsake pieces, where each job uses several filament colors at different price points. It rolls material cost, electricity, waste, and machine wear into one per-unit cost, then feeds that cost into whichever pricing model fits the situation: raw cost, cost plus labor, a retail markup, or a lighter friends-and-family rate.

Key Features

Material

Per-Color Cost

Add any number of filament colors with weight and price per kg, totals sum automatically.

Overhead

Electricity & Wear

Print time converts to hours, applied against configurable electricity and machine wear rates.

Pricing

Four Pricing Models

Cost only, cost plus labor, a retail markup multiplier, and a friends-and-family range.

Orders

Multi-Print Orders

Save several print jobs into one order and see a combined grand total under the active pricing model.
