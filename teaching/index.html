Overview

Introduction to GIS is a continuing-education course within Chesapeake College's Career and Lifelong Learning division, built for adult learners with little to no prior geospatial experience. I built the curriculum from the ground up around QGIS, an open-source alternative to ArcGIS, so students can keep using the software after the course ends without needing a paid license.

What Students Learn

Foundations

Spatial Data Creation

Digitizing points, lines, and polygons, and understanding how spatial data is structured.

Data

Attribute Management

Building and editing attribute tables, joining data, and querying features.

Design

Symbology

Choosing symbology and classification methods that make a map communicate clearly.

Output

Map Production

Laying out and producing a finished, presentable map from raw data.

Format & Reach

Each cohort runs 5-10 adult students, applying GIS skills to real-world, career-relevant scenarios that support workforce development and professional advancement. The course draws from across the college's five-county service area on Maryland's Eastern Shore.
